Short Haircut Trends to Watch in 2026

Soft Pixie

The classic pixie is softer this year, with gentle texture and slightly longer pieces around the ears and nape for a more wearable feel.

Bixie (Bob + Pixie)

The bixie sits between a bob and pixie – shorter in the back, with more length and volume on top. It's one of the standout short hair trend 2026 cuts for those who want something bold but not too extreme.

Italian Bob & Box Bob

These strong, structured bobs remain among the popular short haircuts for 2026, offering a sharp, chic look that works well with both straight and wavy textures.

Cropped Shag

Combining short length with shaggy layers, cropped shags give serious texture and personality. They are perfect if you like the energy of wolf cuts but want shorter hair.
