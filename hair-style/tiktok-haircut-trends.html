Most Popular TikTok Haircut Trends Right Now

While trends move quickly, a few themes keep showing up across viral haircut on TikTok videos in 2026.

Wolf Cut

A layered, shag‑inspired cut that combines volume, texture, and edge. It's one of the most recognizable TikTok haircut trends and works especially well on medium to long hair with natural movement.

Butterfly Cut

This cut gives you two lengths at once – short, face‑framing layers and longer lengths underneath – and is a frequent star in trending haircuts right now videos.

Curtain Bangs

Curtain bangs remain among the most popular hairstyles on TikTok. They are soft, flexible, and look good in ponytails, messy buns, and sleek styles alike.

Italian Bob & Box Bob

Short, sharp bobs with subtle texture dominate many TikTok hairstyle ideas for people craving a strong, chic shape that still feels modern.

Soft Shags & Layers

From micro shags to long, blended layers, textured movement is a major character in trending haircuts right now, especially for those who love a “done but undone” look.

Why TikTok Haircuts Spread So Fast
