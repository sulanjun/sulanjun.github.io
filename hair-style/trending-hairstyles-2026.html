Top Trending Hairstyles 2026 (and Where to Try Them)

Below are some of the biggest haircut trends of the year, along with quick links to their dedicated try‑on pages. Use these to test each look directly on your selfie.

Wolf Cut

The shaggy, layered wolf cut continues to dominate viral hairstyles 2026. It combines volume at the crown with wispy ends and works on wavy or straight hair. Try it on your own photo before you commit.

Butterfly Cut

One of the most requested haircut trends 2026, the butterfly cut uses layered “wings” around the face to create dimension while keeping length. Perfect for those who want a big effect without losing inches.

Curtain Bangs

Soft curtain bangs are everywhere this year, flattering many face shapes and blending easily with mid‑length and long hair. They are a gentle way to join the new hairstyle trends without fully changing your cut.

Soft Bob & Italian Bob

Bobs are among the most popular haircuts this year, especially softly textured or Italian bobs that sit at the jaw or collarbone. They give a polished look with minimal styling.

Face-Framing Layers

2026 is big on movement. Layered haircuts that frame the cheekbones and jaw help add dimension without sacrificing length, and they pair well with many of the other trends on this list.

TikTok Viral Cuts

From micro bangs to modern shags, many viral hairstyles 2026 start on TikTok. Instead of guessing, you can preview TikTok‑famous looks on your own face first.

Short Haircuts 2026

Pixies, bixies, and cropped bobs are among the boldest haircut trends 2026. More people than ever are going short and using AI previews to build courage before the big chop.
