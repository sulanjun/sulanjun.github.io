Common Mistakes to Avoid When Getting Bangs

Before you answer should I get bangs with a yes, avoid these common pitfalls:

❌ Cutting Bangs Yourself

DIY bang trims often go wrong. Even if you see myself with bangs virtually and love the look, visit a professional stylist for the actual cut.

❌ Not Considering Maintenance

Bangs need trimming every 3-4 weeks. If you're not committed to maintenance, you might regret the decision. Try bangs before cutting to ensure you're ready for upkeep.

❌ Ignoring Your Hair Type

Curly, thick, or fine hair all require different bang approaches. What works on straight hair might not suit your texture. Use Glowify to test bangs on my face considering your hair type.

❌ Choosing Wrong for Your Lifestyle

If you're always in a hurry, high-maintenance blunt bangs might not be ideal. Consider your daily routine when deciding should I get bangs.
