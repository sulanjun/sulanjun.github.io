1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information collected automatically

When you use the App, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Device model and device brand
  • Operating system and version
  • Language and country/region
  • IP address
  • App version
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

This information is used to operate, improve, analyze, secure, and troubleshoot the App.

1.2 Photos, Images, and User-Provided Content

The App allows you to select photos or images for text editing and AI-assisted image processing. Depending on your Android version and the feature you use, the App may request access to selected photos, media, device storage, or the system photo picker.

We use the images you select only to provide the editing functionality you request, such as analyzing an image, editing or replacing text, generating an edited result, previewing the result, or saving/exporting the edited image.

Images you choose for an AI-assisted feature may be transmitted securely to remote servers or third-party/cloud processing providers solely to perform the requested image-processing operation. We do not use your selected photos for independent advertising or marketing by us.

The App does not require access to your entire photo library when Android provides a system mechanism that lets you select only the images you want to use.

1.3 Camera access

If the App offers a camera option and you choose to use it, camera permission is used only to capture an image for editing. The App does not access the camera in the background for unrelated purposes.

2. AI and Cloud Image Processing

Some editing features may rely on remote AI or cloud-based image processing. When you start one of these features, the image you selected and the editing instructions necessary to complete your request may be sent to a processing provider.

Such processing may involve:

  • Uploading the selected image for temporary processing
  • Analyzing image content needed to perform the requested edit
  • Generating or modifying pixels in the requested text area
  • Returning the processed result to the App

Processing providers may handle data on our behalf under their own infrastructure, security controls, contractual terms, and applicable legal obligations. We do not intentionally submit your images for unrelated advertising purposes.
