1. Scope

This policy covers Catdoku (the "Game") as published on Google Play. The publisher is Zeqiu Wang, an individual developer based in the People's Republic of China. You can reach us at wzeqiu@163.com.

The Game has no accounts and no login. We do not keep player data on a server of our own.

Google Play, Android and the service providers named below handle store, device and service information under their own policies. That processing is outside this policy, and the relevant service shows you its own privacy notice where its rules require one.

2. What is involved

The Game gives rise to the categories below. None of them pass through a server we operate.

We do not receive names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses or identity numbers, because the Game has nowhere to enter them. If the platform runs its own age or identity checks, that is the platform's processing and we do not see the result.

The Android package declares these permissions: android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID. You can revoke them in Android system settings at any time.

3. What we do not collect

  • Name, e-mail address, phone number, postal address, identity numbers
  • Contacts, call logs, messages or chat content
  • Precise location
  • Photos, camera or microphone content
  • Clipboard, keystrokes, installed apps
  • Social accounts and friend lists
  • Card or bank details
  • Health and biometric data

4. Advertising

Ads in the Game are served by Google AdMob. Before the advertising SDK starts, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may request or refresh consent where the law requires it. AdMob processes device information, the advertising identifier, IP address, and impression and click records to serve and measure ads and detect invalid traffic. See Google's Privacy Policy.

The Game supplies no player profile and gives AdMob no in-game behaviour records. It requests child-directed treatment and caps ad content at rating G, so ads are not personalised using this audience's advertising identifier.

You can reset or delete the advertising identifier in Android system settings. Clearing app data also removes locally stored consent state where the platform permits it.

5. Local storage

Your save sits in the runtime's local storage under a single key: level progress, booster counts and toggles. It holds nothing that identifies you as a person.

The Game sets no tracking cookies and does no device fingerprinting. To delete the save, clear the app data in Android Settings, or uninstall the Game. We keep no copy, so progress cannot be restored afterwards.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information and do not pass it to third parties for their own purposes. Two cases involve others:

  • Google Play, AdMob and Firebase — they process store, runtime, analytics, crash and advertising data under their own policies in order to distribute the Game, provide diagnostics and serve ads.
  • Authorities or courts — only what a valid legal request requires.

We do not publish your information. If the business is transferred, the recipient stays bound by this policy.

7. Cross-border transfers

We are based in the People's Republic of China, but the Game has no backend of ours: your save never leaves the device, and we carry out no transfer of personal data ourselves.

The platform and the advertising service may process the runtime and advertising data described above on servers in other countries, under their own safeguards. If we ever need to transfer data out of your country ourselves, we will rely only on recognised mechanisms such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses, and you can ask us for details.

8. Retention and deletion

Except where the law requires otherwise, we keep information only as long as the purpose needs it. Here that means:

  • The save stays on your device until you clear the data or uninstall; there is no copy on our side to delete.
  • Runtime, analytics, crash and advertising data are kept by Google and the advertising service for the periods their policies state.
  • E-mails you send us are kept only while we answer them and keep a record, then deleted.

To delete everything, clear the app data in Android Settings, or uninstall the Game. Your local progress goes with it.

9. Security

The Game collects no identity data, so our systems hold no player records to steal. Assets and ad requests are fetched over HTTPS, and the client contains no key that could reach personal data. The save carries a version number and is validated field by field: damaged or edited values are discarded, so they cannot freeze the Game or duplicate a reward.

If a security incident affects you, we will tell you what happened, what it may mean for you and what you can do, and report it to the competent authority as the law requires.

10. Your rights

You can ask us to confirm what we hold, and to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to its processing, receive a portable copy, or withdraw consent at any time.

In this Game those rights work like this:

  • Access and correction — level, boosters and toggles are visible in the Game and update as you play.
  • Deletion — clear the app data in Android Settings, or uninstall the Game; we hold no other copy.
  • Advertising — reset or delete the advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • By e-mail — write to wzeqiu@163.com. We reply within 30 days. We may ask for the minimum needed to confirm the request concerns you.

California residents may also ask what categories we collect, request correction or deletion, opt out of sale or sharing, and be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. We do not sell information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

If our reply does not satisfy you, you may complain to your local data protection authority or bring the matter before a competent court.

11. Children

The Game is rated 8 and over. It has no chat, no user-generated content, no purchases and no links out to other sites. We ask for no personal information, so we do not knowingly collect anything about a child.

On advertising: we give the advertising service no player profile and do not ask it to run interest-based ads for children.

Parents and guardians can write to us to check or delete anything relating to their child. Since we keep no server copy, clearing the data on the device removes it completely. Players under 18 should use the Game with a parent or guardian.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the Game's features, our providers or the law change, and mark the new date at the top of the page.

If a change materially affects what we handle or why, we will tell you in the Game or on the store listing before it takes effect, and ask for consent again where the law requires it. If you disagree with an update, you can stop playing and clear the local data.
