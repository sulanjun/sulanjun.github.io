Why Players Love Catdoku

1500 Cat Logic Puzzles

Start with friendly 4x4 boards and work your way up to challenging 12x12 puzzles. Every level is built around one small deduction at a time.

One Cat per Row, Column and Color

The rules are simple: every row, every column and every color block gets exactly one cat. Cats never touch each other, not even diagonally.

Pure Logic, No Math

There are no digits to calculate. Read the colors, clear the impossible cells and let each cat find its place through deduction.

Game Features

Simple Tap and Drag Controls

Tap a cell to mark it empty, drag across several cells at once, or double tap to place a cat. A short hands-on tutorial explains every gesture.

Smart Hints System

Use a hint when you are stuck, or let Find Cat clear the obvious cells. The game helps you keep moving without spoiling the whole solution.

Progress That Saves Itself

Each level gives you three lives, and your progress saves automatically. Close the app whenever you need to and continue from the same board later.

How to Play

Step 1: Analyze the Colors

Look at the board and find each color block. Every block needs exactly one cat, so start with the rows, columns and areas that are easiest to read.

Step 2: Mark and Place

Tap to mark cells that cannot hold a cat. When a cell is certain, double tap to place a cat and watch the nearby options disappear.

Step 3: Complete the Board

Keep going until every row, column and color block has one cat. With no cats touching, the finished board is a perfect little cat puzzle.
