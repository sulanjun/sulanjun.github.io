Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Catdoku: Cat Sudoku Puzzle ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, Google AdMob policies, GDPR, and applicable U.S. privacy laws.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information collected automatically

When you use the App, the App and its service providers may automatically process certain technical information, including:
  • Device model and device brand
  • Operating system and version
  • Language and country
  • IP address
  • App version
  • Crash logs and diagnostics
  • Network type
  • Interaction events
  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID), where permitted by law and device settings
This information is used to operate, improve, and secure the App.

1.2 Game Progress Data

The App stores your game progress, puzzle state, remaining lives, score, boosters, achievements, and settings locally on your device. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. Catdoku does not require an account or cloud save feature.

1.3 Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, camera or microphone content, precise GPS location, health information, biometric information, payment card details, or social account information.

2. Advertising and Analytics

2.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. AdMob may collect and use:
  • Advertising Identifier (AAID), where permitted
  • IP Address
  • Approximate Location derived from IP address
  • Device information
  • App usage and ad interaction data
  • Diagnostic information
AdMob may use this information to deliver ads, apply frequency limits, measure ad performance, and prevent invalid traffic. Catdoku is intended for players aged 8 and over. We configure ad requests for child-directed treatment, limit ad content to a G rating, and do not request interest-based advertising or remarketing. Google's privacy practices: https://policies.google.com/privacy How Google uses partner data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2.2 Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

We use Google Firebase services to understand how users interact with the App, improve performance, and fix errors. Firebase may collect:
  • Usage statistics
  • Session and app interaction information
  • Device and app version data
  • Crash information and diagnostics
Crashlytics collection is disabled in debug builds and enabled in production builds. We use this information to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the App.

3. EU Users (GDPR) - Consent via Google UMP

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland:
  • We request consent where required before using personal data for advertising.
  • We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent framework.
  • You can accept or decline available consent choices.
  • You can withdraw consent anytime through the available privacy controls.
The App checks consent status before initializing the advertising SDK. If consent does not allow ad requests, the App continues without ads and does not grant an ad reward. Legal basis under GDPR:
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)) for personalized advertising and advertising identifiers
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) for basic app operation, analytics, diagnostics, fraud prevention, and security
  • Legal obligation where processing is required by applicable law

4. United States Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA / State Laws)

If you are a resident of California, Colorado, Virginia, Connecticut, or Utah, you may have the right to:
  • Know what personal information we collect
  • Request access to or a copy of personal information
  • Request correction of personal information
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Opt out of sale or sharing of personal information
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information
  • Be free from discrimination for exercising your privacy rights
We do NOT sell personal information. Advertising services may be considered "sharing" under California law. Catdoku advertising is configured for child-directed treatment and is not used for interest-based advertising. You may control advertising choices by:
  • Using the in-app consent form where available
  • Resetting or deleting your Advertising ID in Android settings
  • Contacting us using the details below

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for players aged 8 and over. It contains no chat, user-generated content, social features, purchases, or links that invite children to submit personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. We configure advertising for child-directed treatment and do not request interest-based advertising. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will review the request and take appropriate action. Players under 18 should use the App with a parent or guardian.

6. Data Retention

We retain collected data only as long as necessary to:
  • Provide app services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Maintain security and app stability
Local game progress remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. We keep no separate copy. Advertising, analytics, and crash information are retained by Google according to its policies and our service configuration.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. The App does not maintain a developer-operated account database, and network requests made by integrated Google services use encrypted connections. Local save data is validated and versioned so damaged values can be discarded safely. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (Consent Management)
  • Google Firebase Analytics (Analytics)
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics (Crash Reporting)
  • Google Play (App Distribution and Platform Services)
These providers may process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own privacy policies.

9. Your Choices

You can control data collection by:
  • Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App to delete local progress
  • Resetting or deleting the Advertising ID in Android settings
  • Using the consent form presented by Google UMP where available
  • Limiting personalized ads or resetting advertising settings in your Google account
  • Contacting us to ask about access, correction, or deletion
