Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Avatar & Cartoon Maker ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Privacy at a glance You choose which photo to use. A selected photo is uploaded only when you request an AI avatar or cartoon transformation. You can contact us to request deletion of data associated with your guest account.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Photos and generated images

The App lets you select a photo from your device to create an AI avatar or cartoon-style image. When you start a generation request, the selected photo is prepared on your device and uploaded to our cloud storage and AI processing providers. The photo, selected style, and generation instructions are used to perform the transformation you requested.

Generated images are returned to the App and may be downloaded to private app storage on your device. If you choose to save a result to your photo library or gallery, that copy is managed by your device.

We do not use your photos for facial recognition, identity verification, or to create biometric identifiers for our own purposes. We do not use your selected photos for our own advertising.

1.2 Guest account and device identifiers

You do not need to provide a name or email address to use the App. The App creates a guest account using:

  • the Android Advertising ID (AAID), when available and permitted; or
  • a randomly generated installation identifier stored on your device.

We also process a guest user ID, a security token, device identifier type, and related timestamps. These values help restore your tasks, enforce generation limits, protect requests, and keep one user's images separate from another user's images. The full security token is stored locally; our server stores a one-way hash used to authenticate it.

1.3 Generation and service records

We process records needed to operate the service, such as the selected template, task status, object references for uploaded and generated files, generation source, provider task identifier, error codes, and creation or completion timestamps.

1.4 Device, network, advertising, and diagnostic data

When you use the App or its third-party services, technical information may be processed automatically, including:

  • device model, operating system, app version, language, and region;
  • IP address, network information, and security logs;
  • Advertising ID, consent status, ad impressions, and ad interactions, where permitted;
  • basic request, performance, error, and diagnostic information.

This information may be processed by hosting, advertising, app-store, or AI service providers under their own privacy terms.

1.5 Support communications

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, along with any information you voluntarily provide.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create the AI avatar or cartoon transformation you request;
  • upload, process, deliver, and temporarily store selected and generated images;
  • create and secure your guest session;
  • restore active tasks and previously downloaded results on your device;
  • apply free-use, rewarded-ad, or subscription eligibility rules;
  • display rewarded advertisements and manage advertising consent;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the App and its backend;
  • prevent abuse, fraud, duplicate submissions, and unauthorized access;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests.

3. Photo and Media Permissions

The App uses the system photo picker or photo-library access so you can choose a source photo. It may also request permission to add a generated image to your photo library or gallery when you select the save feature. On older Android versions, storage permission may be used only for compatible gallery-saving behavior.

We do not access your entire photo library when the operating system lets you choose specific photos. You can revoke photo, media, or storage access at any time in your device settings, although this may prevent photo selection or saving.

4. AI and Cloud Processing

Avatar generation requires remote processing. When you request a transformation:

  1. the selected image is resized and converted to a JPEG on your device;
  2. the image is uploaded over an encrypted connection to private cloud storage;
  3. a third-party AI image-generation provider receives a temporary image link and the instructions needed to create the selected style;
  4. the generated result is copied to our private cloud storage and delivered to your authenticated App session.

These providers process the photo and related request information only as needed to provide, secure, and support the requested service, subject to their infrastructure, agreements, retention practices, and legal obligations.

5. Advertising and Consent

The App uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded advertisements. Watching a rewarded ad may unlock an eligible generation or allow a generated image to be saved to your gallery.

Google and its partners may process the Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information, consent status, ad impressions, and interactions. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required to request advertising consent and provide privacy choices. You can revisit available advertising privacy options from the App.

6. Purchases and Subscription Information

If purchases or subscriptions are offered in the App, payment is handled by Google Play or another applicable app store. We do not receive your full payment-card details. We may receive and store purchase or entitlement information such as an app user ID, product ID, entitlement status, transaction identifier, and subscription start or expiration time.

A subscription-management provider such as RevenueCat may process purchase and entitlement information if enabled for the App. Its handling of information is governed by its own privacy terms.

7. Service Providers and Information Sharing

We share information only as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, including with:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, networking, serverless processing, database, workflow, security, and object storage services;
  • AI image-generation providers — processing the selected photo and generating requested results;
  • Google AdMob and UMP — rewarded advertising, consent, and advertising privacy choices;
  • Google Play — app distribution and, where applicable, purchase processing;
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management, if enabled;
  • professional advisers, authorities, or other parties when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or complete a business transaction.

We do not sell personal information in the traditional sense. Some advertising disclosures may be treated as "sharing," "targeted advertising," or "sale" under certain U.S. state privacy laws. Where applicable, you can use the App's advertising privacy choices or device settings to limit this processing.

8. Legal Bases for EEA, UK, and Swiss Users

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract or requested service — to create and deliver the avatar you request;
  • Consent — for personalized advertising and other processing where consent is required;
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App, prevent abuse, and provide non-personalized service functions where permitted;
  • Legal obligations — when processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or appeal a denied request.
