How to Play

Step 1: Find Matching Colors

Look for pairs of colored dots on the board. Each color appears exactly twice.

Step 2: Connect the Dots

Swipe from one dot to its matching partner. Draw a pipe connecting them without crossing other pipes.

Step 3: Fill the Board

Connect all color pairs and fill every empty cell on the board to complete the puzzle!
