How to play

Three steps are all you need to start. The rest is learning when to slow down and look one move ahead.

Read the orders

Check the color requests above and around the board. Each order asks for a specific color and amount.

Tap an exposed screw

If the color fits, it goes straight into the order. If not, it moves into one of the limited temporary slots.

Break, sort, reveal

Hammer a plank when the path is blocked, clear every order, and let the hidden picture appear one section at a time.
