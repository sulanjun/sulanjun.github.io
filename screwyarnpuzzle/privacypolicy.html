1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information collected automatically

When you use the App, our advertising provider may automatically collect technical information such as:

  • Device model and device brand
  • Operating system and version
  • Language and country settings
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • Network type and connection information
  • App version, interaction events and session data
  • Android Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Crash logs and diagnostics generated by the App or Google Play services

This information is used to operate, deliver, measure and secure the App, including showing ads and preventing invalid traffic.

1.2 Game progress data

Game progress, settings and collection data are stored locally on your device. We do not operate an account system or our own cloud-save service. If your device has backup enabled, Android may include App data in that device-level backup according to your system settings.

1.3 App permissions

The Android version of the App requests only the following permissions:

  • INTERNET — to load advertisements.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — to check network connectivity.
  • AD_ID — to use the Android advertising identifier where permitted.
We do not request precise GPS location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library access, and we do not collect such data through the App.

2. Advertising and Analytics

2.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App, currently including rewarded video and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect and use:

  • Android Advertising Identifier (AAID)
  • IP address
  • Approximate location
  • Device information
  • App usage data

AdMob may use this information to provide personalized ads or non-personalized ads depending on your consent status and region. You can learn more at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from partner sites and apps.

2.2 Google Analytics / Firebase

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our App, improve performance, and fix errors. Firebase Analytics may collect usage statistics, session duration, device data, app interaction events and crash information.

2.3 Consent framework

We use Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required, to record your choice, and to determine whether ads may be requested. You can withdraw or change consent by using the consent prompt when it is shown, or by resetting your Android Advertising ID in device settings.

The App is supported by advertising and analytics services. We do not claim that the App is ad-free.

3. EU Users (GDPR) — Consent via Google UMP

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we request your consent before using your data for personalized advertising. We use Google's User Messaging Platform consent framework for this purpose.

You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Where available, changing your consent response will change the type of ads shown.

4. United States Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA / State Laws)

Residents of California, Colorado, Virginia, Connecticut, Utah and other U.S. states may have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, correct inaccuracies, opt out of certain advertising-related sharing, and limit the use of sensitive information.

We do not sell personal information in the traditional sense. Advertising technology may be considered "sharing" under California law. You can limit personalized advertising by resetting your advertising identifier or enabling "Delete advertising ID" in Android settings.

To exercise a privacy right, contact us using the address in section 11. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for players aged 8 and above. Where applicable, our advertising configuration treats the App as child-directed and does not use interest-based advertising or build advertising profiles from the Android Advertising ID for that traffic.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through the App, contact us at wzeqiu@gmail.com and we will delete it where required.
