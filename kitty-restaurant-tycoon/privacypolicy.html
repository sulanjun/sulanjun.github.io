Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kitty Restaurant Tycoon ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with applicable privacy laws, including Google Play Developer Program Policies, Google AdMob policies, GDPR, and applicable U.S. privacy regulations.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our App, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Device model and manufacturer
  • Operating system and version
  • Language and country information
  • IP address
  • App version
  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Crash reports and diagnostics
  • Game usage information
  • Network information

This information is used to operate, maintain, improve, and secure the App.

2. Game Data

Your game progress, achievements, restaurant upgrades, and settings may be stored locally on your device.

We do not collect your personal game progress unless you choose to use optional backup or cloud features in the future.

AdMob may collect and process information such as:

AdMob may use this information to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements depending on your consent settings and region.
