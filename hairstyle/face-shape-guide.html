How to Determine Your Face Shape

Before choosing a hairstyle, you need to identify your face shape. Here's a simple method:

  1. Measure your forehead - from hairline to hairline at the widest point
  2. Measure your cheekbones - across the widest part of your cheekbones
  3. Measure your jawline - from the tip of your chin to below your ear
  4. Measure your face length - from hairline to chin

Compare these measurements to determine your face shape:

  • Oval: Face length is greater than width, forehead slightly wider than jaw
  • Round: Width and length are nearly equal, soft angles
  • Square: Strong jawline, forehead and jaw similar in width
  • Heart: Wide forehead, narrow jaw and chin
  • Oblong: Face is significantly longer than wide

Best Hairstyles for Round Face

Round faces have soft angles with similar width and length. The goal is to add height and create the illusion of length.

Best Haircuts for Round Face Men

  • Pompadour - Adds height and elongates the face
  • Faux Hawk - Creates vertical lines and definition
  • Side-Swept Undercut - Adds asymmetry and angles
  • Textured Quiff - Volume on top draws the eye upward
  • High and Tight - Clean sides with length on top

Avoid: Round or bowl cuts, very short buzz cuts, and styles that add width to the sides.

Best Hairstyles for Round Face Women

  • Long Layers - Creates vertical lines and slimming effect
  • Side-Swept Bangs - Adds angles and asymmetry
  • High Ponytail - Elongates the face
  • Textured Bob - Adds movement and dimension
  • Middle Part with Waves - Creates vertical lines

Avoid: Blunt bobs at chin level, heavy straight bangs, and styles that add volume at the sides.

Best Hairstyles for Oval Face

The oval face shape is considered ideal because of its balanced proportions. Almost any hairstyle works well with this versatile shape.

Best Haircuts for Oval Face Men

  • Classic Side Part - Timeless and sophisticated
  • Crew Cut - Clean and masculine
  • Textured Fringe - Modern and stylish
  • Slick Back - Elegant and confident
  • Medium Length Layers - Relaxed and versatile

Tip: Oval faces can experiment with almost any style. Consider your hair texture and personal style when choosing.

Best Hairstyles for Oval Face Women

  • Blunt Bob - Showcases balanced features
  • Long Beach Waves - Enhances natural beauty
  • Chin-Length Pixie - Bold and chic
  • Curtain Bangs - Frames the face beautifully
  • Sleek Straight Hair - Highlights symmetry

Best Hairstyles for Square Face

Square faces have a strong jawline with forehead and jaw similar in width. The goal is to soften angles and add some roundness.

Best Haircuts for Square Face Men

  • Textured Crop - Softens the jawline
  • Side Part with Fringe - Adds asymmetry
  • Quiff with Faded Sides - Balances proportions
  • Medium Length Swept Back - Softens strong features
  • Beard with Longer Hair - Complements strong jaw

Avoid: Very short buzz cuts and severe geometric styles that emphasize the square shape.

Best Hairstyles for Square Face Women

  • Wispy Bangs - Softens the forehead
  • Long Layers with Waves - Adds softness
  • Side-Parted Bob - Creates asymmetry
  • Loose Curls - Softens angular features
  • Textured Lob - Lengthens the face

Best Hairstyles for Heart-Shaped Face

Heart-shaped faces have a wider forehead and narrower jaw. The goal is to balance the wider upper face with the narrower chin.

Best Haircuts for Heart-Shaped Face

  • Side-Swept Bangs - Narrows the forehead
  • Chin-Length Bob - Adds width at the jaw
  • Long Layers - Balances proportions
  • Textured Waves - Adds volume at the bottom
  • Medium Length with Volume - Creates balance

Avoid: Styles that add volume at the crown or very short cuts that emphasize the forehead.

Best Hairstyles for Oblong Face

Oblong faces are longer than they are wide. The goal is to add width and create the illusion of a shorter face.

Best Haircuts for Oblong Face

  • Full Fringe/Bangs - Shortens face length visually
  • Waves or Curls - Adds width at the sides
  • Chin-Length Bob - Creates width
  • Side Part - Breaks up the length
  • Layered Medium Cut - Adds volume and dimension

Avoid: Very long straight hair, high ponytails, and styles that add height on top.
