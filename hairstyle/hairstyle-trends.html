Korean Hairstyles for Men: Short & Stylish

Korean men's hairstyles have taken the world by storm, known for their clean lines, versatility, and effortless cool factor. These styles work particularly well for Asian hair types but can be adapted for various textures.

TRENDING

Korean Two-Block Cut

The two-block is the quintessential Korean men's hairstyle. It features dramatically short sides and back while keeping the top significantly longer. This creates a striking contrast that's both modern and versatile.

  • Best for: Oval, square, and oblong face shapes
  • Styling: Can be worn as fringe, slicked back, or textured quiff
  • Maintenance: Requires regular trims to maintain the contrast
POPULAR

Korean Side-Swept Fringe

This soft, romantic style features longer bangs swept to one side, creating a gentle frame around the face. It's perfect for those who want a more youthful, approachable look.

  • Best for: Oval and heart-shaped faces
  • Styling: Use a light hold product and blow-dry for natural movement
  • Hair type: Works best with straight to slightly wavy hair

Short Korean Textured Crop

A shorter version of the popular crop, this style features cropped sides with textured length on top. It's clean, professional, and requires minimal styling.

  • Best for: All face shapes
  • Styling: Apply texturizing product and mess with fingers
  • Maintenance: Low - perfect for busy professionals

Trending Hairstyles for Men 2026

Beyond Korean styles, 2026 brings a mix of nostalgic revivals and modern innovations. Here are the top trends dominating men's hair this year:

HOT

Modern Mullet

The mullet has made an unexpected comeback, but with a contemporary twist. The modern mullet is softer, more textured, and less extreme than its 80s predecessor.

  • Shorter, more blended sides
  • Textured layers throughout
  • Works with natural waves or curls

Textured Quiff

The quiff remains a timeless classic, but 2026's version emphasizes natural texture over structured height. Think effortless volume with a matte finish.

  • Best with medium to thick hair
  • Use sea salt spray for natural texture
  • Works for both casual and formal settings

Buzz Cut with Design

For the bold, buzz cuts with shaved designs are making waves. From geometric patterns to subtle lines, this style is all about personal expression.

  • Ultra-low maintenance
  • Perfect for hot weather
  • Requires confidence to pull off

Medium Flow

Longer men's hair continues to trend, with the "flow" style gaining popularity. This surfer-inspired look features medium-length hair with natural movement.

  • Requires patience to grow out
  • Best with naturally wavy hair
  • Low daily styling required

Popular Haircuts for Women 2026

Women's hairstyles in 2026 embrace individuality, with options ranging from ultra-short pixies to long, layered looks. Here are the most sought-after styles:

TRENDING

The Modern Bob

The bob continues to reign supreme in 2026, with variations to suit every face shape and hair texture. From sleek and sharp to tousled and textured, there's a bob for everyone.

  • French Bob: Chin-length with bangs, effortless chic
  • Textured Bob: Layers for movement and volume
  • Asymmetrical Bob: One side longer for edge
  • Blunt Bob: Clean lines, minimal layers

Curtain Bangs

Curtain bangs remain hugely popular, framing the face beautifully while being incredibly versatile. They work with almost any hair length and texture.

  • Softens facial features
  • Grows out gracefully
  • Works with straight, wavy, or curly hair

The Shag

The 70s-inspired shag is back with a vengeance. This layered cut adds incredible volume and movement, perfect for those wanting a rock-star vibe.

  • Best for medium to thick hair
  • Works beautifully with natural waves
  • Low maintenance styling

Short Haircuts for Women Over 50

Short hairstyles are particularly flattering for women over 50, offering elegance, ease of styling, and a youthful appearance. Here are the best options:

Classic Pixie Cut

The pixie is timeless and ageless. A well-cut pixie can take years off your appearance while being incredibly easy to maintain.

  • Highlights cheekbones and eyes
  • 5-minute styling routine
  • Works with fine to medium hair

Textured Lob (Long Bob)

The lob offers the perfect balance between short and long. It's sophisticated, versatile, and can be styled numerous ways.

  • Falls between chin and collarbone
  • Can be worn straight, wavy, or curled
  • Flattering for all face shapes

Layered Short Cut with Side Bangs

Layers add movement and volume, while side bangs soften the forehead and frame the face beautifully.

  • Adds volume to thinning hair
  • Easy to style with a round brush
  • Versatile for day or evening looks

Low Maintenance Hairstyles

For those who prefer to spend less time styling, these low-maintenance options deliver great looks with minimal effort:

For Men

  • Buzz Cut: Zero styling required, just regular trims
  • Crew Cut: Clean, professional, minimal product needed
  • Textured Crop: Wash, apply product, go
  • Caesar Cut: Short all over with forward fringe

For Women

  • Wash-and-Go Bob: Air dries beautifully with natural texture
  • Long Layers: Minimal styling, maximum movement
  • Natural Curls: Embrace your texture with the right cut
  • Short Pixie: Quick finger styling is all you need
