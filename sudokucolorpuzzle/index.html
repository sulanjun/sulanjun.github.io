How to Play

Step 1: Number Rules

Follow classic Sudoku rules: each row, column, and 3x3 box must contain numbers 1-9 without repetition.

Step 2: Color Rules

In addition to numbers, each color must also be unique within every row, column, and 3x3 box.

Step 3: Solve the Puzzle

Place numbers and colors strategically. Use logic to find the unique solution for each puzzle!
